  • storing your entries and syncing them between your devices and the devices of the partner you linked the shared space with;

4. What actions with the data you allow

Collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, use, transfer (granting access to the partner you linked your space with, and cross-border transfer — see clause 5), blocking, deletion, destruction. Processing is carried out by mixed means — both automated and non-automated.

5. Cross-border transfer

You consent to the cross-border transfer of your personal data: it is stored and processed on Google servers (Google LLC, Google Ireland Limited) using the Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage services, located outside the Russian Federation. Google acts as an infrastructure provider and processes the data on the data controller's behalf; the terms are in the Firebase Privacy and Security policy.

6. Who gets access to the data

Your entries are available to you and to the one partner you linked the shared space with using an invite code. By linking the space, you grant your partner access to the entries in it. Other users of the app cannot access your data. As the owner of the Firebase project, the data controller technically has administrative access to the database and uses it only to fix failures and to comply with legal requirements.

7. Duration of the consent and how to withdraw it

The consent is valid for as long as your account exists, or until you withdraw it.

You can withdraw the consent at any time:

  • by deleting your account in the app: Us → Couple Settings → Delete Account;
  • by writing to kellerdimka@gmail.com.

After withdrawal, processing stops and the data is deleted as described on the account deletion page: if you are alone in the space, everything is deleted; if you are linked with a partner, the shared entries stay with them, because they belong to both of you. Withdrawing consent makes using the app impossible — it does not work without storing your entries.

8. Changes to this text

If this text changes, a new edition with a new number will be published, and the app will ask you to confirm your consent again.
