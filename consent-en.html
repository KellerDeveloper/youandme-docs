Consent to personal data processing

You&Me · Edition 2, September 19, 2026

By ticking the consent box when signing in to You&Me, you confirm that you have read this text and the privacy policy, and you give the data controller your consent to process your personal data on the terms below.

In short
  • We store what you put into the app yourself — so that it syncs between your devices and your partner's devices.
  • The data lives in Russia, on Yandex Cloud.
  • Only what is needed for signing in and for notifications leaves the country: Apple and Google receive the author's name and the kind of event, but not the content.
  • Nobody sees it except you and the one partner you invited.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account in the app.

2. What data is processed

  • the identifier of your Apple or Google account and your email address, received when you sign in with "Sign in with Apple", "Sign in with Google" or by email with a code from the message (the address may be Apple's private relay address);
  • your name, date of birth and gender, if you provided them, and the app language;
  • the files you upload to the app, and their contents;
  • the entries you create: wishes, tasks, lists, notes, plans and trips with expenses, calendar events, important dates and birthdays of loved ones, documents, loyalty cards, time capsules, polls, answers to the question of the day, shared photos;
  • the addresses of your devices used to deliver notifications, the platform and the app version;
  • technical logs of requests to the server, including the IP address — for no longer than three days.

The app does not collect biometric or special categories of personal data, does not determine your location and does not use the advertising identifier.

When you enter someone else's data — a loved one's name and date of birth, a mention in a note or a file — you are responsible for making sure that person does not mind.

3. Purposes of processing

  • storing your entries and syncing them between your devices and the devices of the partner you linked your shared space with;
  • access to your entries without an internet connection;
  • notifications about your partner's actions;
  • signing in to your account and restoring access to it;
  • keeping the service working and secure;
  • responding to your support requests.

The data is not used for advertising, behavioral analytics, profiling or transfer to third parties.

4. Actions you permit

Collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, use, transfer (granting access to the partner you linked your space with, entrusting processing to the parties named in clause 5, and cross-border transfer in the volume described in clause 6), blocking, deletion, destruction. Processing is carried out by mixed means — both automated and non-automated.

5. Where the data is stored and who processes it

The data is stored and processed in the Russian Federation, on Yandex Cloud, zone ru-central1: the YDB database, files in Object Storage, the server on Cloud Functions and API Gateway, sign-in code emails through Cloud Postbox. Yandex Cloud processes the data on the data controller's behalf and may not use it for its own purposes.

Until September 2026 the data was stored on Google servers (Firebase) outside Russia. Storage has now been moved to Russia, and copies in Firebase are being deleted as the migration completes.

6. Cross-border transfer

You consent to the cross-border transfer of your personal data in a limited volume and only for two purposes:

  • signing in — an exchange with Apple Inc. and Google LLC when verifying the signed sign-in token;
  • delivering notifications — Apple Inc. (Apple Push Notification service) receives the name of the partner who made the entry, the kind of event and a link to the section, and for a shared photo a link to that photo; Google LLC (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Android only) receives the section name, the kind of event and the entry identifier — without any name and without any content.

The content of your entries, your files, documents and capsule letters are not transferred outside the Russian Federation. Without this consent, notifications and signing in with Apple or Google cannot work.

7. Who gets access to the data

Your entries are available to you and to the one partner you linked your shared space with using an invite code. By linking the space you grant that partner access to the entries it contains. Other users of the app cannot access your data. As the owner of the cloud project, the data controller technically has administrative access to the database and uses it only to fix failures and to comply with legal requirements.

8. Duration of the consent and how to withdraw it

The consent is valid for as long as your account exists, or until you withdraw it.

You can withdraw it at any time:

  • by deleting your account in the app: Us → Couple Settings → Delete Account;
  • by writing to kellerdimka@gmail.com.

After withdrawal, processing stops and the data is deleted as described on the account deletion page: if you are alone in the space, everything is deleted; if you are linked with a partner, shared entries stay with them, because they belong to both of you. Withdrawing the consent makes using the app impossible — it does not work without storing your entries.

9. Changes to this text

If this text changes, a new edition with a new number will be published, and the app will ask you to confirm your consent again. That is how edition 2 came about: data storage moved from Google to Russia, so we are asking again for consent that was given on different terms.
