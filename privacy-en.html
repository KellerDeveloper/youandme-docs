6. Updating, correcting, deleting and destroying personal data

6.1. Rights of the personal data subject:

The personal data subject has the right to:

  • receive information concerning the processing of their personal data;
  • demand that the Operator clarify, block or destroy their personal data in cases provided for by law;
  • withdraw consent to the processing of personal data;
  • appeal against the actions or inaction of the Operator to Roskomnadzor or in court;
  • exercise other rights provided for by Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data".

6.2. Procedure for exercising rights:

To exercise their rights, the personal data subject sends a written request to the Operator at the email address kellerdimka@gmail.com or to the Operator's address.

The request must contain the information provided for by Article 14 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data".

The Operator undertakes to consider the request and provide a response within a period not exceeding 10 (ten) working days from the date of receipt of the request. This period may be extended by no more than 5 (five) working days, provided that the personal data subject is sent a reasoned notice stating the reasons for the extension.

6.3. Destruction of personal data:

6.3.1. The Operator ceases processing, blocks, clarifies and destroys personal data in the manner and within the periods provided for by Article 21 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data":

  • when the purpose of processing is achieved — ceases processing and destroys the personal data within a period not exceeding 30 calendar days, unless there are other legal grounds for further processing;
  • when the subject withdraws consent — ceases processing and destroys the personal data within a period not exceeding 30 calendar days, if its retention is not required for the purposes of processing and there are no other legal grounds;
  • upon receipt of the subject's demand to cease processing — ceases processing within a period not exceeding 10 working days; this period may be extended by no more than 5 working days by sending the subject a reasoned notice;
  • upon receipt of a complaint about unlawful or inaccurate processing — blocks the relevant personal data from the moment of the complaint for the period of verification;
  • upon confirmation that the personal data is inaccurate — clarifies it within 7 working days and lifts the blocking;
  • upon detection of unlawful processing — ceases it within a period not exceeding 3 working days, and if it is impossible to ensure lawful processing, destroys the personal data within a period not exceeding 10 working days;
  • if destruction within the established period is impossible — blocks the personal data and ensures its destruction within no more than 6 months, unless another period is established by federal law.

6.3.2. Confirmation of the destruction of personal data is:

  • for processing without automation tools — a certificate of destruction of personal data;
  • for automated processing — a certificate of destruction of personal data together with an extract from the event log of the personal data information system.

6.3.3. These documents (certificates of destruction and event log extracts) are kept by the Operator for 3 (three) years from the moment the personal data is destroyed, in accordance with the requirements of regulatory legal acts of the Russian Federation, including Roskomnadzor Order No. 179 of 28.10.2022.
