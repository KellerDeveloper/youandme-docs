Privacy Policy

You&Me · Edition 2, September 19, 2026

You&Me is a private space for two: shared plans and trips, documents at hand, wishes, notes, loyalty cards, a calendar of important dates. This policy explains what the app stores, where that data lives, who can see it and how to delete it.

The app is built and maintained by Dmitry Alekseevich Keller (the "data controller", "we"). For any question about this policy or your data, write to kellerdimka@gmail.com.

In short
  • Your data is stored in Russia, on Yandex Cloud.
  • We do not sell your data and do not show ads.
  • The app has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • Your entries are seen only by you and the one partner you linked your space with.
  • You can delete your account and data from within the app at any time.

1. What data we collect

Account data

There are three ways to sign in: Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google and by email address — a one-time code is sent to your inbox. From Apple and Google we receive the permanent identifier of your account with them and an email address; if you chose "Hide My Email", it will be Apple's private relay address. The app has no passwords, and neither do we — not in plain text, not encrypted.

Sign-in methods can be linked and unlinked in settings. The email address also lets us recognise you: sign in with Apple once and with Google another time using the same address, and you end up in the same account rather than in two separate ones.

Profile

Your name, date of birth and gender, if you provided them, and the app language. The date of birth is used only to show your birthday inside the shared space and to remind your partner about it in advance. Gender is used so the app addresses you and your partner with the right words.

Entries you create

Everything you put into the app is stored so it can sync between your devices and your partner's devices:

  • wishes, including product links, prices and attached pictures;
  • tasks and shopping lists;
  • notes and note folders;
  • plans and trips, including their expenses;
  • shared calendar events, important dates and birthdays of loved ones;
  • documents and files you upload or scan, and the files themselves;
  • loyalty cards, including card numbers and barcodes;
  • time capsules — letters sealed until a date you choose;
  • polls and your votes;
  • the question of the day and your answers to it;
  • shared photos you add to the home screen.

The app does not require you to upload identity documents, bank details or other sensitive information, and does not recommend doing so. What exactly goes into the shared space is up to you; access to what you upload is restricted to the members of your space — the server verifies membership on every read and every write.

Other people's data that you enter

In the loved ones section and in the shared calendar you can record other people's names and dates of birth, and in notes and files you can mention anyone. Such data is processed as content of your space. Entering someone else's personal data is your decision and your responsibility: make sure that person does not mind.

Device permissions

  • Camera — to scan pages and read loyalty card barcodes. The pictures are saved to your space and sent nowhere else.
  • Calendar — to import events from the device calendar and export shared events back. This happens on the device and only when you ask for it.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — to lock the files section. The check is performed by iOS itself; the app only receives a "yes" or "no" and has no access to your biometrics.
  • Photos — the pictures you choose are handed to the app by the system picker. The app does not get access to your whole library.
  • Notifications — to tell you what your partner has done. How that works is covered in section 5.

Links you paste

When you attach a product link to a wish, the app opens that page in the background to read the title, price and picture. The request goes directly from your device to the site you pasted and is governed by that site's rules. We keep no log of opened links — only the wish itself is saved in your space.

Technical data

To deliver notifications we store the addresses of your devices (push tokens) issued by Apple or Google, along with the platform name and app version — they show what is installed on the device and where a notification should go.

The server keeps technical logs of requests: IP address, time, method path, response code, the client string (user-agent) and error text. They are used only to investigate failures and to protect against abuse, and are kept for no longer than three days — after that they are erased automatically.

What we do not collect

The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party trackers. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask for tracking permission. It does not collect your location.

2. Where the data is stored

The data is stored and processed in the Russian Federation, on Yandex Cloud, in the ru-central1 zone — that is, in Russian data centres. Four of its services are involved:

  • Yandex Managed Service for YDB — the database: profiles, couples and all your entries;
  • Yandex Object Storage — files: documents, scans, wish pictures, shared photos;
  • Yandex Cloud Functions and API Gateway — the server itself, through which the app reaches the data;
  • Yandex Cloud Postbox — sending the code email when you sign in by email.

Yandex Cloud processes the data on our behalf as an infrastructure provider and may not use it for its own purposes.

Copies of your files are additionally kept in the app's private folder on your device, so they can be opened without an internet connection. That folder is inaccessible to other apps and protected by the device itself.

3. What happened to the data that was in Firebase

Until September 2026 the app ran on Google Firebase, and the data was stored on Google servers outside Russia — as the previous edition of this policy stated. In September 2026 we moved storage to Russia, to Yandex Cloud.

While version 1.4 remains in the App Store, the app keeps duplicating records to Firebase, and the files of the Documents, Wishes, Notes and Photos sections are stored in Google Cloud Storage. The reason is technical: your account identifier was issued by Firebase, and the same identifier sits inside your couple's earlier records. Were we to switch the duplication off today, a partner on the old version would stop seeing shared records and would lose access to their account.

The duplication ends together with support for version 1.4, after which the copies of the data in Firebase are deleted and the files are moved to Yandex Cloud. What still runs through Google, and in what volume, is in section 5.

4. Who can see your data

Your entries can be read only by a member of your space: you and the one partner you linked with using an invite code. The code is six characters long, lives for 15 minutes and works once. Membership is verified by the server on every read and every write — this is not hiding things in the interface, but a server-side check without which the request simply does not go through.

Some entries — capsule letters, your votes and your answers to the question of the day — are protected further: the server hands them over only once the section's own rules allow them to be opened.

Let's be straight: as the owner of the cloud project, the data controller technically has administrative access to the database. It is used only when necessary — to investigate a failure or to comply with a legal requirement — and never to read your content out of curiosity.

Apart from those we entrust with processing — the next section names them — we pass the data to nobody. We do not sell it, rent it out or use it for advertising.

5. Who processes data on our behalf

Besides Yandex Cloud, Apple and Google take part in the app's operation — and only in two places: signing in and delivering notifications. Here is the honest scope of what each one receives.

Apple

Sign in with Apple: you sign in on Apple's side, the app receives a signed token from them, and we verify the signature. Apple knows that you signed in to You&Me.

Notifications on iPhone and iPad go straight to the Apple Push Notification service, bypassing Google. A notification carries the name of the partner who made the entry, the kind of event ("New task", "New plan") and a link to the section; if it is a shared photo, also a link to that photo, which the phone loads itself. The content of your entries is not included: not the text of a task, not the title of a wish, not a capsule letter.

Google

Sign in with Google works the same way as Sign in with Apple: a token from Google, the signature verified by us.

Notifications on Android can technically only be delivered by Firebase Cloud Messaging — otherwise the system will not wake a closed app. So we use FCM as a signal, not as a letter: the message carries only the section name, the kind of event and the entry identifier. No name, no text, no title — the phone composes the notification itself, after fetching the data from our server in Russia.

Yandex Cloud

Storing data and files, running the server and sending the sign-in code email — see section 2.

6. Legal basis and purposes of processing

We process the data solely to run the features you use: syncing your entries between your devices and your partner's devices, giving you access to them offline, notifying you about your partner's actions and answering your support requests.

Where the GDPR applies, the basis is performance of a contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) — the service you are asking for cannot exist without storing what you put into it. For users in Russia the basis is your consent and performance of the contract — see section 11.

7. Retention and deletion

The data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete the account right from the app: Us → Couple Settings → Delete Account. What exactly gets deleted — on the account deletion page.

In short: if you are alone in the space, everything is deleted. If you are linked with a partner, your profile and your membership are deleted, while the shared archive stays with your partner, because it belongs to both of you. In both cases the local copies of files on your device are erased.

Data is removed from the live database immediately. Residual copies may persist for some time in the cloud infrastructure's backups until they are overwritten. Technical logs erase themselves within three days.

8. Your rights

You can request access to your data, its correction, blocking or deletion. The fastest way to delete is from the app, as described above. For everything else, write to kellerdimka@gmail.com — we will answer as quickly as we can. If you are in the EEA or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are in Russia — with Roskomnadzor.

9. Children

You&Me is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new edition will be published on this page with a new date and number at the top. We will announce significant changes inside the app and ask you to confirm your consent again.
