Privacy Policy

You&Me · Edition 1, August 25, 2026

You&Me is a private space for two: shared plans, files, wishes and loyalty cards. This policy explains what the app stores, where that data lives, who can see it and how to delete it.

The app is built and maintained by Dmitry Alekseevich Keller (the "data controller", "we"). For any question about this policy or your data, write to kellerdimka@gmail.com.

In short
  • We do not sell your data and do not show ads.
  • The app has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • Your entries are seen only by you and the one partner you linked your space with.
  • You can delete your account and data from within the app at any time.

1. What data we collect

Account data

You can only sign in with Sign in with Apple on iPhone or Sign in with Google on Android. From there we receive a user identifier and an email address — if you chose "Hide My Email", it will be Apple's private relay address. We store the account identifier issued by Firebase Authentication. There are no passwords: we never see or store any.

Profile

Your name and date of birth, if you provided them. The date of birth is used only to show your birthday inside the shared space and to remind your partner about it in advance.

Entries you create

Everything you put into the app is stored so it can sync between your devices and your partner's devices:

  • wishes, including product links, prices and attached pictures;
  • plans, trips, tasks and votes;
  • shared calendar events and important dates;
  • time capsules — letters sealed until a date you choose;
  • loyalty cards, including card numbers and barcodes;
  • files you upload or scan, and the files themselves.

The app does not require you to upload identity documents, bank details or other sensitive information, and does not recommend doing so. What exactly goes into the shared space is up to you; access to uploaded files is restricted to the members of your space by server-side rules.

Device permissions

  • Camera — to scan pages and read loyalty card barcodes. The pictures are saved to your space and sent nowhere else.
  • Calendar — to import events from the device calendar and export shared events back. This happens on the device and only when you ask for it.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — to lock the files section. The check is performed by iOS itself; the app only receives a "yes" or "no" and has no access to your biometrics.
  • Photos — the pictures you choose are handed to the app by the system picker. The app does not get access to your whole library.

Links you paste

When you attach a product link to a wish, the app opens that page in the background to read the title, price and picture. The request goes directly from your device to the site you pasted and is governed by that site's rules. We keep no log of opened links — only the wish itself is saved in your space.

What we do not collect

The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party trackers. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask for tracking permission. It does not collect your location.

2. Where the data is stored

The data is stored with Google Firebase — Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage — on Google's cloud infrastructure. Google processes this data on our behalf as an infrastructure provider; see the Firebase Privacy and Security policy.

The Google servers that run the project are located outside the Russian Federation. What that means is covered in section 10.

Copies of your files are additionally kept in the app's private folder on your device, so they can be opened without an internet connection. That folder is inaccessible to other apps and protected by the device itself.

3. Who can see your data

Your entries can be read only by a member of your space: you and the one partner you linked with using an invite code. This is enforced by security rules on the server that verify membership on every read and write — not just by hiding things in the interface.

Let's be straight: as the owner of the Firebase project, the data controller technically has administrative access to the database. It is used only when necessary — to investigate a failure or to comply with a legal requirement — and never to read your content out of curiosity.

We do not pass the data to any other third parties. We do not sell it, rent it out or use it for advertising.

4. Legal basis and purposes of processing

We process the data solely to run the features you use: syncing your entries between your devices and your partner's devices and giving you access to them offline. Where the GDPR applies, the basis is performance of a contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) — the service you are asking for cannot exist without storing what you put into it. For users in Russia the basis is your consent — see section 10.

5. Retention and deletion

The data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete the account right from the app: Us → Couple Settings → Delete Account. What exactly gets deleted — on the account deletion page.

In short: if you are alone in the space, everything is deleted. If you are linked with a partner, your profile and your membership are deleted, while the shared archive stays with your partner, because it belongs to both of you. In both cases the local copies of files on your device are erased.

Data is removed from the live database immediately. Residual copies may persist for some time in the infrastructure provider's backups until they are overwritten.

6. Your rights

You can request access to your data, its correction or deletion. The fastest way to delete is from the app, as described above. For everything else, write to kellerdimka@gmail.com — we will answer as quickly as we can. If you are in the EEA or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are in Russia — with Roskomnadzor.

7. Children

You&Me is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new edition will be published on this page with a new date and number at the top. We will announce significant changes inside the app and ask you to confirm your consent again.
